Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026 · App: Starlingoverse (Android)

In short: You don't create an account for Starlingoverse. Your learning data stays on your own device and is not sent to our servers. The free version shows Google AdMob ads, which Google operates. Payments are handled by Google Play.

1. Controller

[GELİŞTİRİCİ ADI] ("we"). Contact: [İLETİŞİM E-POSTASI]

2. Accounts and personal information

You don't need to provide a name, email address, phone number or similar information to use the app. We don't collect such information and we don't keep user data on our servers.

3. Data stored on your device

The app stores your chosen goals and daily study time, lesson and exercise history (correct/incorrect answers, hints, review schedule), XP, streak and badge information, and subscription status only on your device. This data is not sent to us. You can delete learning data via Profile → Settings and data → Delete all my data, or remove all local data by uninstalling the app.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and secure ads, Google may process your advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), IP address, device and app information and your interaction with ads. This processing is Google's responsibility; see policies.google.com/technologies/ads and how AdMob uses data.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and other relevant regions, the app shows Google's consent form (UMP) on first launch; you can choose your ad preferences there and change them at any time. You can turn off personalized ads or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

5. Subscriptions and payments (Google Play)

Purchasing, renewing and cancelling the Premium subscription is handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. The app only learns from Google Play whether your subscription is active and keeps that status on your device.

6. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (ads) – Google LLC
  • Google Play Billing (subscription payments) – Google LLC

We use no other analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.

7. Children

Starlingoverse is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child is using the app, contact us.

8. Retention and security

Your learning data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Because we keep no server-side user data, there is no server-side breach risk; we recommend using your device's screen lock and updates.

9. Your rights (GDPR / Turkish KVKK)

You have the right to know whether your personal data is processed, to request information, rectification, erasure and to object. Because we don't collect personal data, most rights are exercised by deleting the data on your device. For advertising data processed by Google, contact Google. For other requests: [İLETİŞİM E-POSTASI]. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
